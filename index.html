Book Chapter/Conference Paper MARTECH 2026 · Lisbon, Portugal · May 2026

A clustering evaluation for bulk cargo ports in Brazil using K-Means

Show abstract Hide abstract ▼

Abstract: This study proposes a clustering-based methodology to evaluate and classify Brazilian dry bulk terminals using the k-means algorithm implemented in Orange Data Mining software. It considers 2024 data on agricultural commodities and employs nine features that represents the terminals and vessels characteristics. The number of clusters selection is evaluated from two to ten, identifying that three clusters provide the best results based on the higher silhouette score. The results reveal distinct performance among clusters. The least efficient includes most of the terminals, is characterized by lower rankings across most features except for queueing times. The median cluster hosts 77% of its terminals in the south region. Lastly, the most efficient is highlighted in consignation, throughput, productivity, and berth time. Furthermore, notably, the Santos-Guarujá Port Complex hosts the highest-performing terminals, with seven among the ten top-ranked in the most efficient cluster.

R. B. Pieper and T. P. Tancredi, in Innovations in Maritime Technology and Engineering, 1st Edition, vol. 2, London: CRC Press, 2026. DOI 10.1201/9781042001194-98

Conference Paper XXIX COPINAVAL · Lima, Peru · September 2025

Forecasting Brazil’s maritime container throughput: A comparative study of SARIMA, and LSTM Neural Network models with cyclical encoding.

Show abstract Hide abstract ▼

Abstract: Brazil's container volume has shown steady growth, reaching a 20% year-over-year increase in 2024. This trend, along with planned investments in the port sector, highlights the need for accurate forecasting to support strategic planning and operational efficiency. This study evaluates forecasting models for Brazil's container throughput using public data from ANTAQ’s Waterway Statistical Panel. Two main approaches are compared: the Seasonal Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average (SARIMA) and the Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) neural network. The impact of lag selection and cyclical encoding in LSTM is also explored through feature engineering. Python is used for model development, with pandas, numpy, statsmodels, and tensorflow libraries. Results show that LSTM outperforms SARIMA in forecasting, achieving an average combined training and test MAPE of 5.76% and RMSE of 61.54 thousand TEU, further improved to 5.15% and 60.45 thousand TEU with cyclical encoding.

R. B. Pieper and T. P. Tancredi, in Proceedings of the 29th Pan-American Congress of Naval Engineering, Maritime Transportation and Port Engineering (COPINAVAL), Springer Nature, Sep. 2025.

Conference Paper XII CIDESPORT · Florianópolis, Brazil · November 2025

Teoria das Filas no Planejamento Portuário: Um estudo de caso com o terminal de contêineres de Salvador

Show abstract Hide abstract ▼

Abstract: The expansion of international trade is directly linked to port activity, as a significant share of goods traded worldwide is transported by vessels and handled through ports. This paper aims to assess the vessel-handling capacity of the Salvador Container Terminal through the application of Queueing Theory, with a focus on operational planning studies. Using publicly available data from ANTAQ and the terminal, the berth-operation subsystem was modeled considering a real-world scenario and different hypothetical scenarios. The single-queue, multiple-server M/M/c model was used to evaluate classical queueing performance metrics and service-level indicators based on approaches proposed in the literature. The results indicate that, considering a maximum waiting time of 24 hours and a maximum service-level threshold of 0.3, the terminal can handle between 390,000 and 650,000 containers per year in short-term scenarios, depending on the performance metric adopted, and reach between 850,000 and 1.2 million containers per year in long-term scenarios involving investments in productivity and/or the activation of a third berth. The main contribution of this paper lies in the mathematical and statistical modeling approaches presented to support port management, operational analysis, and planning.

R. Pieper, T. Tancredi, and R. Dávalos, in XII Congresso Internacional de Desempenho Portuário, Florianópolis, Nov. 2025, pp. 477–495.
